# Where We Can Win Against OpenChoreo

OpenChoreo provides a strong application platform model for building, deploying, and operating applications. Our opportunity is to differentiate through deeper operational and financial context using BuildPiper, REMS, and SpendSmart.

## Potential Gaps and Opportunities

| Opportunity | Potential OpenChoreo Gap to Validate | How We Can Win |
|---|---|---|
| Unified DevOps + Operations + FinOps | Delivery, health, events, and cost may not be deeply connected in one application view | Connect BuildPiper deployments, REMS events, and SpendSmart costs around the same application |
| Application-to-cost visibility | Cost may be visible at infrastructure level rather than clearly attributed to applications and teams | Show cost by application, environment, deployment, and owner |
| Operational context | An alert may not immediately show which deployment or application change caused it | Link alerts to deployments, releases, environments, and recent changes |
| Actionable agents | SRE and FinOps agents may provide guidance, but depth of automated action needs validation | Build agents that investigate issues, explain impact, recommend actions, and safely execute approved changes |
| Enterprise adoption | Platform setup and infrastructure requirements may create adoption friction | Provide easier onboarding, existing enterprise integrations, and gradual adoption |
| Executive visibility | Technical platform views may not fully answer business and financial questions | Provide leadership views for health, risk, reliability, ownership, and spend |
| Migration experience | Moving existing applications into the platform may require significant setup | Support incremental onboarding without forcing teams to change their entire toolchain |
| Closed-loop optimization | Monitoring and cost recommendations may remain separate from delivery workflows | Turn insights into actions such as redeployment, scaling, configuration changes, or ownership workflows |

## Recommended Focus

The strongest opportunity is not to build another deployment platform.

It is to build an application operating platform that answers:

> What changed, is the application healthy, what is it costing us, and what should we do next?

## Winning Statement

> OpenChoreo helps teams build and run applications. We can win by helping teams build, run, understand, and continuously improve applications across delivery, reliability, and cost.
